      About Joe

      Joe Trimmeliti has spent a lifetime making images — and has recently rekindled his first love: painting.

      Joe graduated from George Brown College in 1974 in Graphic Design and spent fifteen years as a Senior Staff Artist in the Toronto Public Library’s art department. Today he is self-employed in sign making and graphic design, and paints from King City, Ontario.

      His oil and acrylic paintings are original impressions inspired by his natural surroundings and rich imagination. They have been warmly embraced by the art enthusiasts and collectors who have attended his shows, and he has been successful in several fine art shows and sales.
